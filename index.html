Why it works

  1. Frame. Magic bytes, an optional AES-GCM block with a PBKDF2-SHA256 key, a length and a CRC-32.
  2. Soak. Each byte becomes eight bits, and each bit replaces the low bit of one colour channel. A channel moves by one step out of 256.
  3. Carry. The picture survives screenshots, messaging apps and PNG re-encodes. JPEG re-encoding, resizing or cropping destroys the payload — the message is as fragile as the file.

Vision models receive a re-encoded, downsampled copy of the image, so the low bit never reaches them. That is the whole point of the paper: hiding in plain sight is easy, seeing it is not.
